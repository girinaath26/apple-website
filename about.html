Our Story

Started in a garage. Changed the world.

In 1976, Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne started Apple in a small garage in Cupertino, California. What began as a passion project — a personal computer for everyone — grew into one of the most influential companies in history.

From the original Macintosh to the iPod, iPhone, iPad and beyond, Apple has always believed that great technology should be simple, beautiful, and accessible to everyone.

Today, Apple serves over a billion active users across the world — and that number keeps growing. But our mission has never changed.
